SENDING GUIDE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Send day:       Tuesday or Wednesday morning (highest open rates)
- Subject lines:  Sound like a person, not a campaign
- From name:      AutoCleanse (not "AutoCleanse Marketing")
- Batch split:    57 contacts day 1, 58 contacts day 2 (free tier)
- Review before:  Each job spotlight - make sure the figures are real
- Image policy:   Add a photo to job spotlights if you have one.
                  DPF/Revenue/Remap emails work well text-only.

TONE RULES (non-negotiable)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Short sentences. Always.
  UK spelling: tyre, colour, authorised, kerb.
  No: revolutionary, cutting-edge, seamless, bespoke, solutions.
  No: "In today's fast-paced world..."
  Numbers as numerals: 1 hour, 3 days, £200.
  Talk to a busy workshop manager. Respect their time.
  One point per email. That's it.
