When a forced regen
isn't enough.

A forced regen burns off soot. It doesn't remove the ash, oil residue, or cerium oxide that builds up over time. That's why some vehicles come back within weeks.

Off-vehicle cleaning removes what a regen can't. We flush the filter under pressure, clear the channels, and test flow before and after. The difference shows up in the readings.

If you've got a vehicle that keeps triggering the DPF light after regens, it's worth a proper clean before anyone starts talking about replacement.

We send back a before/after pressure drop report with every job. Useful if a customer wants evidence the clean was worth doing.
